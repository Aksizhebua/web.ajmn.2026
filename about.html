ABOUT US

PT ATMA JAYA MITRA NUSANTARA

PT Atma Jaya Mitra Nusantara is a professional company specializing in building management and event services. Supporting Universitas Katolik Indonesia Atma Jaya and the wider community, we provide reliable solutions in facility management, event organization, business support, education, community engagement, and AV training services, including seminars, certification programs, and brevet AB courses to enhance professional competencies.

Our commitment to excellence and innovation makes us a trusted partner for various institutions looking to optimize their operations and create meaningful experiences.

Our Vision

Enhancing human dignity through excellent, professional, and caring building management and event services, while supporting education, research, training, and health services based on Christian values.

Our Purpose

  • SERVICE EXCELLENCE To provide professional, reliable, and high-quality building management and event services in support of academic, business, and community activities.
  • RESOURCE OPTIMIZATION To optimize the utilization of facilities and resources through innovation and operational efficiency.
  • STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS To build strategic partnerships with various stakeholders in order to create added value for the university, society, and the business sector.
